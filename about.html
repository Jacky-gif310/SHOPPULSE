ABOUT SHOPPULSE

Understanding Shopping Experiences Through Data

ShopPulse transforms customer feedback into meaningful insights, helping users explore and compare shopping experiences across different platforms.

OUR PURPOSE

One Place to Compare Customer Experiences

Customers use different shopping platforms every day, but their experiences can vary considerably. ShopPulse brings these experiences together so they can be explored and compared using data.

The platform collects customer feedback about areas such as overall experience, product satisfaction, delivery, pricing, customer support and willingness to recommend.

The collected responses are transformed into visual comparisons and easy-to-understand insights, making it easier to identify patterns, strengths and areas that may need improvement.

📊

From Feedback to Insight

Customer responses become structured data that can be compared, visualized and interpreted.

HOW IT WORKS

From Customer Experience to Clear Comparisons

01

Share

Customers provide feedback about a shopping experience on a platform they have used.

02

Analyze

Responses are organized and analyzed across important customer experience metrics.

03

Compare

Results are compared across different shopping platforms to reveal meaningful differences and patterns.

04

Understand

Visualizations and automated insights make the findings easier to understand and use.

WHAT WE MEASURE

Key Customer Experience Areas

Overall Experience

How customers rate their overall shopping experience.

Product Satisfaction

How satisfied customers are with the products they receive.

Delivery

Customer perception of delivery quality and speed.

Pricing

Whether customers consider prices fair and reasonable.

Customer Support

How customers evaluate the support they receive.

Recommendation

Whether customers would recommend their shopping platform.

💡

Who Can Benefit?

The insights can support consumers, researchers, students, small businesses and online sellers who want to better understand customer expectations.

WHY IT MATTERS

Better Understanding Leads to Better Decisions

For consumers, comparative feedback can provide a broader view of shopping experiences across platforms.

For small businesses and online sellers, understanding customer expectations can help inform decisions about service, delivery, product quality, pricing and support.

ShopPulse does not represent or speak on behalf of the shopping platforms being compared. It is an independent analytics project based on the customer responses collected through the platform.
